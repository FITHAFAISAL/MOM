How to Capture Device & Meeting Audio

To transcribe speech from other attendees in Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, YouTube, or any app playing sound on your device:

1
Choose Audio Source:

Select "Both: Mic + Device Audio" (captures you + meeting attendees) or "Device Audio Only".

2
Click "Start Listening":

Your browser will show a sharing selection prompt.

3
Enable Audio Sharing:

• If sharing a Chrome/Edge Tab (e.g. Google Meet / YouTube): Ensure "Share tab audio" is checked.
• If sharing Entire Screen (e.g. Zoom or Teams desktop app): Turn on "Also share system audio" toggle at the bottom.

4
Real-Time Detection & MOM:

Both your voice and incoming meeting speech will stream directly into the text box. When finished, click "Prepare MOM" to generate your meeting minutes!
